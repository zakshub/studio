# Research Policy

## Objective
Expand reusable production intelligence without turning trend-following, client taste, or source popularity into universal truth.

## Research selection
Prefer sources that can add:
- new production method
- new visual causality insight
- new material/camera/light behavior
- new workflow evidence
- useful contradiction
- meaningful provider benchmark evidence

Reject research that is only:
- fashionable
- visually attractive
- duplicate
- source-poor
- unverifiable
- client-specific without broader relevance

## Observation discipline
Separate:
1. observed fact
2. interpretation
3. proposed reusable rule

Never present interpretation as observed fact.

## Source quality
Prefer primary/official/creator documentation when available. Secondary summaries may support discovery but should not outrank direct evidence.

## Brand neutrality
Named brands may appear as research sources. Their identity, aesthetic, slogans, campaign language, or visual signature must not become default Studio behavior.

Promote only generalized production insight.
