# Focused Visual Research 2026 10 01

Access date 2026 10 01

Six datasets were selected only after comparison with Studio rules EF 001 through EF 022.

## Diesel FW26 Lets Go
Brand Diesel. Photographer Verity Smiley Jones plus local photographers.
Selected for two stage image construction. Models were photographed in studio, converted to life size cardboard cutouts, shipped to cities, then photographed again in real local environments.
New learning: physical rephotography can make a composite layer visibly truthful because it inherits real perspective, contact, ambient contamination and local capture artifacts.
Classification: high value rule. Promote EF 023.

## Alaia Winter Spring 2026
Brand Alaia. Photographer Steven Meisel.
Selected for deliberate repetition of one frontal composition. The stable frame removes environmental novelty so silhouette, body and garment construction become easier to compare.
New learning: fixed frame repetition can work as a comparative instrument rather than only visual consistency.
Classification: new and repeatable. Promote EF 024.

## Jil Sander FW26
Brand Jil Sander. Photographer and director Moni Haworth.
Selected because MiniDV and VHS like low fidelity is concept bearing rather than treated as a defect.
New learning: capture fidelity is a semantic design variable. Reduced fidelity is valid when its artifacts reinforce the story while critical material readability survives.
Classification: new and repeatable. Promote EF 025.
Rejected sub finding: domestic furniture on airport tarmac overlaps EF 011 semantic environment collision.

## Burberry The Trench Portraits of an Icon
Brand Burberry. Photographer Tim Walker.
Selected because one iconic garment remains central while identity changes through small wearing behaviors.
New learning: collar position, belt treatment and other micro styling variables can carry identity while product recognition stays fixed.
Classification: new and repeatable. Promote EF 026.

## Balenciaga Icon Photocards Campaign
Brand Balenciaga. Photographer Hannah Moon.
Selected because portraits become collectible graphic objects. Images are cut and collaged on white paper stock to resemble narrative panels and collector cards.
New learning: a campaign photograph can gain a second semantic layer by visibly becoming a physical or graphic artifact when the transformation is concept led and product readability survives.
Classification: high value rule. Promote EF 027.

## Gucci Primavera 2026
Brand Gucci. Photographer Michal Chelbin.
Selected because historical visual grammar is used without turning contemporary clothing into costume.
New learning: borrow historical pose, spacing, gaze and light grammar separately from period wardrobe to create temporal depth while keeping fashion current.
Classification: new and repeatable. Promote EF 028.

## Rejections
Jil Sander displaced furniture and tarmac contrast duplicates EF 011.
Dior Lady Dior natural light, heritage setting and spontaneous posing overlaps EF 018.
AMIRI interrupted conversations and implied narratives overlap EF 015.
Givenchy Croatia terrain response overlaps EF 014 and EF 001.
Generic grain, natural light, rough versus refined contrast and candid posing were not promoted.

## Representative frames
No PNG screenshots were committed. The accessible research interfaces exposed rendered pages and image references but did not provide a reliable direct original image binary extraction path with clear reuse status. Source links are preserved in sources.md and no frames were fabricated.
