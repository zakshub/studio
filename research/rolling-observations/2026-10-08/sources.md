# Source Registry, Focused Visual Research

Access date: 2026-10-08
Research folder: research/rolling-observations/2026-10-08/

## 1. Moncler Have a Puffy Summer, Summer 2026
Platform: official Moncler website.
Creator: Moncler. Set designer Andy Hillman and team are named; photographer not stated.
Source: https://www.moncler.com/en-gb/have-a-puffy-summer-campaign
Selection reason: sculptural animal props translate the collection's puffy, layered product language into the set.
Limitations: the page was accessible, but linked image CDN endpoints failed during retrieval.

## 2. Valentino Spring Summer 2026, Fireflies
Platform: MAI Productions portfolio and Fashion Gone Rogue.
Creator: Valentino. Photographer and director Willy Vanderperre; art director Christopher Simmonds; stylist Jonathan Kaye; set designer Victoria Salomoni.
Sources: https://maiproductions.com/works/valentino-spring-summer-2026 ; https://www.fashiongonerogue.com/story/valentino-spring-2026-campaign/
Selection reason: an arrested pre fall moment offers a useful exception to motion based garment validation.
Limitations: campaign stills viewable; no EXIF, lens, exposure, or LUT data published.

## 3. Phoebe Philo Collection D 2026
Platform: MAI Productions portfolio.
Creator: Phoebe Philo. Photographer Talia Chetrit; creative director Phoebe Philo; set designer Jabez Bartlett.
Source: https://maiproductions.com/works/phoebe-philo-collection-d-2026
Selection reason: mixed image roles, including portrait, product still life, dog, textile and shadow, are bound by repeated campaign typography.
Limitations: several stills viewable; no EXIF or declared grade data published.

## 4. Fendi Fall Winter 2026 to 2027
Platform: MAI Productions portfolio.
Creator: Fendi. Photographer Jo Ann Callis; creative director Maria Grazia Chiuri.
Source: https://maiproductions.com/works/fendi-fall-winter-2026-27
Selection reason: mirrors and foreground figures create simultaneous spatial planes inside one frame.
Limitations: multiple stills viewable; camera and lens metadata not published.

## 5. Balenciaga A New York Minute, 2026
Platform: official Balenciaga website.
Creator: Balenciaga. Directed by Celine Song, starring Sarah Pidgeon. Additional campaign imagery credited to Pierpaolo Piccioli, Monaris and Zora Sicher.
Source: https://www.balenciaga.com/en-jp/a-new-york-minute
Selection reason: a nested film narrative repeats the same hero bag across story layers before revealing the production setup.
Limitations: official description accessible; embedded films not extracted for frame by frame analysis. The page states that three films are each 60 seconds and cover morning, afternoon and evening in Manhattan.
