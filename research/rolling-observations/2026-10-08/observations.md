Visible stills show a historic interior, deep shadows, window light, warm stone, and bodies held in unstable or arrested positions. ## Phoebe Philo Collection D 2026
Visible stills include reflective eyewear in cool blue light, a bag on warm amber leather, a reclining model, a dog on a rocky coast, orange textile, and a hand shadow behind fabric. Repeated red typography overlays these different image types. Candidate learning: graphic continuity can bind portraits, products, animals, and abstract frames despite changes in subject and palette. Classification: new but weak or one off. Defer because EF 015 and EF 027 cover adjacent principles. No EXIF or LUT found.

## Fendi Fall Winter 2026 to 2027
Visible stills use a large mirror to show a seated model while another figure occupies the room outside the reflection. Other frames use a tipped chair, decorated interiors, and tight crops on neckwear. Warm lamps coexist with dark room zones and brighter window light. Candidate learning: a mirror can create a second spatial plane. Classification: new but weak or one off. Defer because evidence is one campaign and partly overlaps EF 021.

## Balenciaga A New York Minute 2026
The official page describes three 60 second films across morning, afternoon and evening in Manhattan. A pan out reveals another set and crew, while the same Le City bag appears in the main story and an inner romantic comedy. Daypart progression overlaps EF 010; production provenance overlaps EF 009. New candidate CF 001: a recurring hero object can preserve product focus across nested story layers. Classification: high value candidate, not promoted. The films were not extracted, so motion observations rely on official text.
