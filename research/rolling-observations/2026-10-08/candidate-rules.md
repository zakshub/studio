# Candidate Rules for Validation

## CF 001 Nested narrative anchor continuity

Status: high value candidate, not promoted to canonical Studio intelligence.

Hypothesis: when a campaign film deliberately moves between a primary story, inner fiction, and a production reveal, a recurring hero object can preserve product focus while apparent reality changes.

Evidence: the official Balenciaga A New York Minute page describes a film within a film and states that the inner romantic comedy protagonist carries the same Le City bag as the main character before a wider reveal exposes another set and crew.

Why it may be useful: the object provides a stable commercial anchor while the audience moves between different levels of staged reality.

Why it is not yet canonical: only one campaign was found that clearly demonstrates this exact mechanism. The governed learning policy requires more evidence before promotion.

Validation needed: find an independent campaign film using a recurring hero object across nested narrative layers, then compare whether the object genuinely improves continuity rather than merely repeating branding.
