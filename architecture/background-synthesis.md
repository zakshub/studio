# Background Synthesis Cycle

## Purpose
Consolidate recent experience without interrupting active production work.

## Inputs
- recent episodic memories
- practice outcomes
- QC failures
- sensory observations
- expert-research observations
- unresolved contradictions
- recent user approvals/rejections

## Cycle
1. collect recent eligible records
2. normalize terminology
3. deduplicate evidence
4. cluster related observations
5. identify repeated success/failure patterns
6. detect contradictions
7. detect missing coverage
8. form scoped hypotheses
9. compare against canonical knowledge
10. create learning candidates or research questions
11. never auto-promote universal knowledge

## Outputs
- candidate abstractions
- contradiction reports
- curiosity/research questions
- contamination/bias alerts
- deprecation candidates
- benchmark candidates

## Schedule
Run asynchronously on a configured cadence and after sufficiently large evidence batches.
