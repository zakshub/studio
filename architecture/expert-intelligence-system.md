# Expert Intelligence System

## Goal
Represent selected experts as distinct specialist knowledge profiles and combine them through councils and meta-intelligence.

## Expert profile
Each expert profile should contain:
- expert_id
- name
- domains
- geography/context
- time periods/phases
- documented methods
- recurring decision principles
- technical practices
- problem-solving patterns
- strengths
- known limitations/context boundaries
- source registry
- extracted reusable principles
- anti-copy constraints
- confidence/evidence quality
- last reviewed

## Critical rule
Learn transferable principles and decision methods. Do not create a mechanism for copying a living expert's signature style.

## Councils
Initial councils:
- photography
- fashion photography
- cinematography
- camera/lens
- lighting
- color/LUT
- editing
- graphic/digital design
- art direction
- creative direction
- fashion/garment
- storytelling
- fine art/perception

## Consultation
A task may request one or more councils.

Example:
Fashion campaign video ->
fashion/garment + cinematography + lighting + editing + color + creative direction.

## Aggregator
The aggregator:
1. selects relevant experts
2. keeps domain-specific weighting
3. preserves disagreements
4. produces synthesized principles
5. checks against world model and task constraints
6. does not reveal private synthesis mechanics to ordinary users

## Evidence discipline
Every expert-derived claim must be traceable to a source or marked as inference.
