# Contamination and Coverage Monitor

## Purpose
Detect whether the universal intelligence is becoming dominated by one client, creator, geography, market, source type, aesthetic family, provider, garment class, or medium.

## Monitor dimensions
- workspace/client origin
- geography
- market level
- garment/product category
- source authority
- creator/expert
- visual medium
- production method
- lighting family
- camera family
- aesthetic cluster
- executor/provider

## Signals
- source concentration
- repeated near-duplicate evidence
- one expert influencing unrelated domains
- client-specific preference appearing in universal candidates
- geographic imbalance
- category blind spots
- stale/obsolete technical knowledge
- one provider's artifacts being mistaken for visual principles

## Actions
- warn
- lower candidate confidence
- request independent evidence
- open curiosity/research question
- block universal promotion
- recommend targeted coverage expansion
