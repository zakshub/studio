# Metacognition and Curiosity

## Metacognition
The system should inspect the quality of its own decision state before action.

### Checks
- evidence diversity
- source authority
- contradiction count
- unresolved uncertainty
- over-reliance on one expert/source/geography
- mismatch between confidence and evidence
- missing domain consultation
- context leakage from another workspace
- novelty vs derivation risk
- whether current plan violates a higher-level value

### Outputs
- confidence adjustment
- missing-evidence requests
- required council consultation
- human-review escalation
- research question
- proceed/hold recommendation

## Curiosity
Curiosity identifies knowledge gaps that are worth reducing.

### Triggers
- repeated uncertainty
- recurring QC failure
- contradictory expert/source evidence
- weak geographic/category coverage
- new pattern appearing across independent sources
- user tasks repeatedly requiring absent knowledge
- model/executor degradation

### Research-question shape
- question
- why it matters
- evidence already known
- evidence missing
- affected domains
- expected information gain
- urgency
- proposed source classes

## Rule
Curiosity may open research work automatically when policy permits, but research results remain candidates until validation/promotion.
