# Governed Learning Loop

## Goal
Continuously improve Studio Brain without uncontrolled drift.

## Inputs
- scheduled research
- manual research
- repeated production outcomes
- QC failures
- user approvals/rejections
- provider benchmark results
- newly observed production techniques
- contradictions with existing rules

## Candidate lifecycle
OBSERVED -> NORMALIZED -> COMPARED -> CLASSIFIED -> VALIDATED -> PROMOTED or REJECTED

## Candidate classifications
- already known
- duplicate phrasing
- new but weak
- new and repeatable
- high-value
- contradiction
- provider-specific
- task-specific only
- unsafe/unverified
- deprecated

## Promotion requirements
A durable rule should be:
- reusable beyond one brand/client
- supported by sufficient evidence
- non-duplicative
- compatible with physical/production reality
- scoped to where it applies
- assigned confidence
- traceable to sources or repeated task evidence

## Anti-drift rule
Client preferences, one campaign, one model's quirks, or a fashionable visual trend must not become universal truth without generalization and validation.

## Research cadence
Scheduled research may autonomously create candidate reports. Promotion into canonical production intelligence remains governed.

## Contradictions
Do not erase the older rule silently. Record scope, evidence, and conditions under which each observation applies.

## Deprecation
Rules may be deprecated when repeated evidence shows they are obsolete, provider-specific, or incorrect. Preserve history in the changelog.
