# Anti-AI Photographic Realism

## Objective
Do not merely make an image "pretty." Remove synthetic cues while preserving the intended photographic class.

## Common AI tells
- plastic or wax skin
- identical pore texture everywhere
- dead/symmetrical eyes
- malformed or overly elegant hands
- impossible object contact
- random garment folds
- perfect semantic cutout edges
- subject floating from background
- abrupt fake Gaussian blur
- impossible perspective/compression
- inconsistent light direction
- highlights with no source
- reflections that do not match the world
- every material sharing the same sheen
- global over-sharpening
- detail that ignores distance/focus
- random film grain overlay
- fake flare/bloom
- showroom-perfect environments
- decorative haze
- overdone orange/teal grading
- hyper-clean "8K" look inappropriate to the medium

## Anti-AI correction principles
1. Fix physical logic before adding texture.
2. Prefer non-uniform detail hierarchy.
3. Preserve ordinary imperfections appropriate to the reality class.
4. Make shadow/highlight sources traceable.
5. Make cloth/hair/body respond to gravity/contact/motion.
6. Keep optical behavior continuous through depth.
7. Differentiate material response.
8. Preserve capture and delivery limitations.
9. Do not use lens/camera names as decoration.
10. Do not solve realism with grain, blur, or LUT alone.

## Edge realism
Edges vary by cause:
- hair: irregular/fine/translucent
- fabric: soft/fuzzy depending on material
- motion edge: directional blur
- focus edge: optical softness
- backlit edge: glow/flare interaction
Avoid uniformly crisp boundaries.

## Detail realism
Real detail is hierarchical:
- highest near focus and hero material
- lower away from focal plane
- lower with distance and atmosphere
- altered by sensor processing, noise reduction, resize and compression

## Human realism
Verify:
- asymmetry
- weight-bearing
- joint plausibility
- hand/object contact
- eye direction
- neck/shoulder logic
- facial micro-expression
- hairline and flyaways

## Fashion realism
Verify:
- exact garment identity when locked
- seams/embroideries remain structurally coherent
- folds arise from gravity/tension/contact
- fabric sheen matches material
- dupatta/scarf/hair respond to same airflow

## Final anti-AI question
Would a skeptical photographer be able to explain this frame through a plausible camera, lens, light, subject, material, environment, post, and delivery chain?
If not, reject or revise.
