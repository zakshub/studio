## Problem-solving model
1. define whether the task needs intimacy, experimentation or clarity
2. remove production noise when direct subject connection matters
3. allow the subject meaningful agency
4. test alternatives instead of defaulting to a familiar formula
5. review accumulated images/references as a memory field
6. let new work alter future perception
