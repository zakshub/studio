## Documented decision principles

### See latent character before the subject sees it
Type: stated_by_expert + observed
Evidence: SM-001, SM-003
Principle:
Casting is not merely selecting conventional beauty. Look for latent character, attitude, transformation potential, and visual presence that can be developed through the shoot.

### Transformation can be the assignment
Type: observed + stated_by_expert
Evidence: SM-001, SM-003
Principle:
A fashion portrait can construct a heightened character rather than simply record the person as found.

### Search deeply before shooting quickly
Type: collaborator-observed
Evidence: SM-004
Principle:
Do not confuse efficiency with immediate capture. Spend time resolving look, styling, character, and alignment; once the image logic is found, execution can become fast and decisive.

### Client constraints do not remove creative responsibility
Type: stated_by_expert
Evidence: SM-002, SM-005
Principle:
Even when clothing or assignment inputs are not inherently inspiring, the image-maker remains responsible for finding a way to make the result compelling and useful.

### Meaning can coexist with fashion utility
Type: stated_by_expert
Evidence: SM-002
Principle:
A fashion image can satisfy the commercial/editorial brief while also carrying an idea, observation, wit, or social meaning.

### Build shorthand through collaboration
Type: observed + stated_by_expert
Evidence: SM-001
Principle:
Repeated collaboration can create a shared visual language in which direction becomes increasingly economical and intuitive.

## Problem-solving model
1. read the assignment and fashion objective
2. identify the person/character with the strongest transformation potential
3. search for the visual look until subject, styling, setting and attitude align
4. use play and collaboration to unlock performance
5. execute decisively once the image logic is found
6. preserve both fashion readability and a larger idea when the brief allows

## Transferable intelligence
- casting is an active creative decision

- commercial usefulness and conceptual meaning can coexist
