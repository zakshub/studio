## Rules
- Experts may belong to multiple councils.
- Council membership is domain-weighted, not equal voting.
- Council output is advisory evidence for the meta-intelligence.
- Disagreement is preserved when both positions are valid in different contexts.
- No council is allowed to override source preservation, rights, safety, or other higher-authority invariants.
