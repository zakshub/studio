# Forensic Reality QC

Score each 1–5. Any score <=2 on preservation, physical causality, anatomy, camera/lens realism, or lighting realism requires rework.

## A. Preservation
- Identity unchanged when locked
- Outfit/product unchanged when locked
- Pose/composition/environment unchanged when locked

## B. Human realism
- Anatomy plausible
- Hands/contact plausible
- Expression/gaze coherent
- Hair behaves physically
- Skin remains non-plastic

## C. Garment/material realism
- Folds have causes
- Fabric response is material-specific
- Embroidery/seams remain coherent
- Contact/compression/gravity are plausible

## D. Camera/lens realism
- Viewpoint and scale coherent
- Focal-length feel matches perspective
- DOF falls off continuously
- Bokeh/edge behavior plausible
- No contradictory optical effects

## E. Exposure/motion
- Motion agrees with shutter
- Noise/detail agree with ISO/capture class
- Highlight/shadow limits are believable

## F. Lighting
- Every major shadow has a source
- Every major highlight has a source
- Bounce and mixed color are plausible
- Contact shadows anchor objects
- Subject and environment share one light field

## G. Environment
- Scale/perspective coherent
- Objects interact correctly
- Reflections are consistent
- Wear/clutter match the reality class
- Foreground/midground/background depth exists

## H. Color/post
- White balance plausible
- Skin/object color protected
- Grade supports light rather than fighting it
- LUT/film look is restrained
- Sharpening/noise/grain not uniform or excessive

## I. Delivery provenance
- Resize/compression/detail fit final medium
- Social/mobile/cinema/still character is appropriate
- No implausibly pristine final texture

## J. Anti-AI
Reject for:
- plastic skin
- impossible fingers
- random wrinkles
- fake blur
- synthetic cutout edges
- impossible reflections
- inconsistent shadows
- global hyper-sharpness
- over-clean environment
- decorative artifacts used to fake realism

## Approval statement
APPROVE only if the image can be explained as a plausible physical capture and processing chain for its selected reality class.
